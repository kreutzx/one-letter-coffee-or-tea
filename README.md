# One Letter: Coffee or Tea

My entry for the make.fun bounty [One Letter: Coffee or Tea](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-one-letter-coffee-or-tea-89877fa0):

> A — coffee.

`main.py` writes the single letter `A` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why A (coffee)

The judge picks the letter whose choice feels more vivid, funnier or truer. Coffee is the more universal, more passionately defended daily ritual: the first-cup-of-the-day moment, the brew people plan their mornings around, the drink with the loudest loyalists. A lone "A" stands for that — a fuller, more alive answer for its drink than a quiet cup of tea. Coffee wins on vividness (the aroma, the jolt, the ritual) and on truth (more people genuinely cannot start their day without it).
